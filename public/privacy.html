Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Chatimall handles information when you use our website and messaging service. Chatimall is operated under the Chatimall name. For privacy questions or requests, email support@chatimall.com.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: phone number or email address used to sign in, verification information, display name, bio, profile image, public encryption key, preferences, and account activity such as last-seen status.
  • Content you submit: messages, photos, videos, voice notes, files, status updates, channel posts, group information, and marketplace listings you create or share. We also process conversation and delivery metadata needed to operate these features.
  • Contacts, if you choose to sync them: the installed app requests device-contact permission. When you choose contact syncing, names and phone numbers are sent to our service to match people who use Chatimall. You can deny or revoke contact permission in your device settings.
  • Device and notification information: linked-device details, platform, and push-notification tokens when you grant notification permission. These are used to keep your account signed in on linked devices and deliver notifications.
  • Support and security information: messages you send to support, authentication and account-security records, and technical information generated when the service is used.

How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain Chatimall, authenticate accounts, deliver conversations and notifications, match contacts when requested, show content to the audience you select, respond to support requests, protect users and the service, and meet legal obligations.

Message encryption and security

Chatimall supports end-to-end encryption for eligible text messages sent through its supported direct and group messaging flows. This does not mean that every type of information is end-to-end encrypted: uploaded media and files, profiles, contact information, service metadata, and all call-related information are not covered by that text-message feature. Use the in-app encryption and safety-code indicators to understand whether a conversation is protected. We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

When information is shared

  • Information and content are shared with the people, groups, or public audiences you choose. Other users may copy or redistribute content they can access.
  • We use service providers that help host, store, secure, and operate Chatimall, send verification messages, and deliver push notifications. They process information on our behalf as needed to provide those services.
  • If TikTok account linking or sharing is available and you choose to use it, the relevant information is exchanged with TikTok under the permissions you authorize. Login Kit and Display API access is limited to the approved scopes shown during consent, such as basic profile information and public videos. TikTok handles information under its own privacy policy.
  • We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, or service security.

Retention and your choices

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate security, dispute-resolution, and legal purposes. Verification codes are temporary. Retention periods can vary by information type and operational requirements.

You can update profile and privacy settings in Chatimall, control contact and notification permissions on your device, and disconnect a linked TikTok account from Chatimall settings when that feature is available. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information, contact support@chatimall.com. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required by law or for legitimate security purposes. Copies already received by other users may remain with them.

Children and international processing

Chatimall is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us. Information may be processed in countries where Chatimall or its service providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws than your country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Chatimall changes. We will update the date at the top when a revised policy is published. Continued use after an update is subject to the updated policy, to the extent permitted by law.
